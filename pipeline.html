Pitch

Call Script

A tight opener you can use on the phone.

"Hey, is this the owner or manager? My name is Roy. I build modern websites for local smoke shops and I put together a quick demo concept for your store — because I think you could look way stronger online. Would you be open to seeing it?"

If yes: "Perfect. I can text or email the link and walk you through it in under 5 minutes. What's the best place to send it?"

If hesitant: "Totally understand — this isn't a commitment. I just made a rough example so you can see what your shop could look like online."

Quick Objections

  • "We already have a site." — "Makes sense. This is more about showing a stronger, conversion-focused version."
  • "We're busy." — "Understood. I can text the demo and you can glance whenever you get a minute."
  • "Not interested." — "No worries at all. If that changes later, I'd be happy to share it."
